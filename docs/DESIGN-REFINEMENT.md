# ManaCircle visual refinement

> Current direction — 13 October 2026: the owner-supplied Patnam Pakodi reference informed a clearer bold-sans design with compact service rows, a native Eightfold brand presentation and sage/forest section contrast. This supersedes both the early design below and the later serif exploration. See [current brief](REWORK-BRIEF.md) and [verification](REWORK-VALIDATION.md). The remaining text is the historical decision record.

Direction confirmed: refine the existing cream and green identity.

The visitor needs to understand the team’s services, judge its creative approach and prepare a relevant enquiry. Success is a clear path from a service or concept to an accurately preselected project brief.

## Design decisions

- Palette: warm cream `#F7F5EF`, paper `#FFFEFA`, forest `#153F35`, green `#146356`, pale sage `#E3E8DA`, muted text `#59665E`.
- Type: locally served Geist for the website; existing editorial serif treatments stay inside the fictional portfolio concepts. Larger headings, comfortable body copy and fewer tiny labels.
- Layout: a confident split hero with an interactive concept showcase; three useful service families; an asymmetric featured-work section; a distinctive eight-part circle for the team; a quieter, shorter contact form.
- Brand: repeated circle geometry connects to ManaCircle and its eight-person team. The generated Studio Form interior adds variety to the portfolio while preserving explicit concept labels.
- Interaction: user-controlled showcase tabs, service-category anchors, optional form details, consistent keyboard focus and restrained hover feedback. No auto-playing carousel or scroll hijacking.

```text
Header: wordmark                      Services / Work / Team / Contact
Hero:   clear headline + project CTA   [Websites | Apps | Content]
                                      interactive concept preview
Services: Build                       Grow                 Create
Work:   larger website concept        interior concept
Team:   eight-part circle              team story
Process / FAQ / project invitation / substantial wordmark footer
```

Review of the direction: grouping services replaces a long list and makes the ten offers easier to scan. Concept art carries the visual interest; interface controls remain quiet. A different interior image prevents the page from presenting the same tea scene repeatedly. No stock team portraits, invented results or client proof are introduced. Contact delivery stays in its current local-download state until configured.

## Photographic refinement

The owner subsequently requested more natural photographic imagery.
